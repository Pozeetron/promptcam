Privacy Policy for PromptCam

Last updated: October 11, 2026
Developer: Evol
Package: com.evol.promptcam

PromptCam (“the App”) is a camera teleprompter that helps you record video intros with scrolling script text. This Privacy Policy describes how the App handles data on your device.

1. Summary

PromptCam processes camera, microphone, speech-recognition, and gallery permissions only to provide teleprompter recording features. Script text and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not require creating an account to use the App.

2. Data processed on your device

  • Camera video. Used to preview and record video while the teleprompter overlay is shown.
  • Microphone audio. Used when “Record audio” is enabled, and/or when voice-follow mode needs to listen to your speech.
  • Speech recognition. In “By voice” mode, on-device / OS speech services may process audio to estimate where you are in the script and adapt scrolling. Recognition is performed through the operating system speech services available on your device; PromptCam does not operate its own cloud speech account for you.
  • Script text. Your script is saved locally (for example with on-device storage) so it can be restored between launches.
  • Settings. Preferences such as scroll mode, font size, overlay height, mirror text, and recording options are stored locally.
  • Gallery / media library. If you grant permission, finished recordings can be saved to your device gallery.
  • Language. The App uses system language settings to localize the UI and choose a speech language tag.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use your camera feed for advertising.
  • We do not require social login.
  • We do not upload your script or recordings to Evol servers as part of normal App operation.

4. Permissions

  • Camera — preview and record video intros.
  • Microphone — record audio with video and/or follow speech tempo.
  • Speech recognition — match spoken words to the script in voice mode.
  • Media / photos (video) — save completed recordings to the gallery.
  • Keep awake — prevent the screen from sleeping while the teleprompter is open.

You can deny or revoke permissions in Android settings. Some features will be limited if a permission is denied (for example, silent recording without microphone access).

5. Third-party / OS services

Speech recognition may be provided by Google, Samsung, or other system speech packages installed on the device. Those providers process audio under their own terms and privacy policies. PromptCam only requests recognition to power the teleprompter follow feature.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Retention

Scripts, settings, and videos remain under your control on the device (and in your gallery if saved). Uninstalling the App removes App-local storage; gallery videos remain until you delete them.

8. Security

Protect your device with a screen lock and keep Android updated. Because recordings and scripts stay on-device, physical access to an unlocked phone can expose that content.

9. International users

The App may be used worldwide. Processing described above occurs on your device and, for speech recognition, through the speech service available on that device.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Material changes will be reflected before a related Play Store release when practical.
